The AI chat assistant

The chat on this site is an automated assistant powered by artificial intelligence. It is not a person, and it will say so if you ask. A person is always available at (816) 200-3338.

The assistant is there to understand your problem and arrange a callback. Please keep in mind:

  • It can be wrong. Its replies are generated automatically and may be inaccurate or incomplete. Don't rely on them as professional advice.
  • It can't quote a price. Any number it mentions isn't an offer. Your price is confirmed by the technician before work starts.
  • It can't promise an arrival time, same-day service or availability. Scheduling is confirmed by a person.
  • It can't diagnose your plumbing. It may suggest a simple, safe check, but a licensed plumber makes the call.
  • It isn't for emergencies. If water is spreading, shut off your main water valve and call us. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. If anyone is in danger, call 911.

What you type is processed and stored as described in our Privacy Policy. Please use the assistant for its purpose: don't try to make it do unrelated tasks, extract its instructions, or submit abusive or false content. We may limit or end access for misuse.

This guidance isn't a substitute for a licensed plumber, and we aren't responsible for repairs you attempt yourself.
